About RAJAPI-COP Africa

A pan-African network serving youth and climate

Our Mission

RAJAPI-COP Africa is the Network of African Youth Bringing Innovative Projects to the Climate, Biodiversity and Desertification COPs for social justice and gender equality.

Our mission is to mobilize, support and connect young people who design and carry concrete, integrated and transformative solutions to the challenges of climate change, biodiversity erosion, desertification, and social and gender inequalities, in full coherence with the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and the commitments of the Paris Agreement.

Our Flagship Programs

Concrete initiatives for lasting impact

Climate COP – UNFCCC

is the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC / UNFCCC). It brings together each year the signatory States, as well as observers (international organizations, civil society, scientists, youth, private sector), to negotiate, adopt and evaluate international policies to combat climate change.

Learn more

Biodiversity COP

The Biodiversity COP (Conference of the Parties) is the Conference of the Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD), adopted at the Rio Earth Summit in 1992. It regularly brings together States parties, as well as international organizations, scientists, indigenous peoples, youth and civil society actors, to decide on global directions for the conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity.

Learn more

Desertification COP

The Desertification COP (Conference of the Parties) is the Conference of the Parties to the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD), adopted in 1994. It periodically brings together States parties, as well as international organizations, scientists, local communities, youth and civil society actors, to coordinate global action against desertification, land degradation and the effects of drought, particularly in arid, semi-arid and dry sub-humid areas.

Learn more

BRS COPs

The COP on chemicals and waste, commonly called BRS COPs, refers to the Conferences of the Parties to the Basel, Rotterdam and Stockholm Conventions, three legally binding international agreements aimed at protecting human health and the environment from the harmful effects of hazardous chemicals and wastes.

Learn more

WUF – World Urban Forum

The World Urban Forum (WUF) is the main global conference on sustainable urbanization, organized by UN-Habitat every two years. It brings together national and local governments, United Nations agencies, civil society organizations, youth, researchers, urban planners, private sector actors and donors to discuss the challenges and opportunities related to sustainable urban development.

Learn more

UNEA – United Nations Environment Assembly

UNEA is the world's leading decision-making body on environmental issues within the United Nations, created in 2012 under the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP). It brings together: UN Member States, international organizations, scientists, NGOs, youth and civil society actors.
